## Labeling guidelines

One email gets exactly one category and one priority. **Reference date:** "today" is the day the email was received, so "tomorrow" or "by Friday" is counted from that day.

### Priority

Priority is decided by **how soon the recipient must act**. One time scale applies to every kind of email.

| Priority | Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `high` | action needed within **3 days**, or a critical problem regardless of the deadline | meeting tomorrow, bill due in 2 days, failed payment, production incident, password expiring in 2 days |
| `medium` | action needed within **4–12 days** | bill due in 10 days, reply to an invitation by next week, preparation for an event next week |
| `low` | action needed in **more than 12 days**, or no action needed | ads and newsletters, already paid bills, approved vacation, information only |

**Critical regardless of the deadline** means only: failed payments or charges, production incidents and outages, and risk of losing access to an account. Nothing else is `high` without a deadline within 3 days. 

### Category

| Category | Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meeting` | an event with a **specific date or time** that the recipient is expected to attend, online or in person, including invitations waiting for confirmation, rescheduled meetings and social events | client call tomorrow 9:00, sprint planning moved to Thursday, RAG meetup next Wednesday, GTA night on Saturday |
| `invoice` | billing and payments: invoices to pay, payment confirmations, failed or declined payments, and requests to submit your own invoice | pre-order invoice, failed GPU charge, "send your B2B invoice by the 5th" |
| `promotion` | the main purpose is a **purchase or saving opportunity**: discounts, sales, promo codes, limited-time offers | 30% off merch, last chance 50% off a course |
| `other` | everything else, including content newsletters without an offer, alerts, account and service notices, personal messages without an event | LLM Weekly, payment API outage alert, password expiry, CV help request |

**Tie-breakers**, when an email fits more than one category:

1. A failed or due payment is always `invoice`, even inside a promotional email.
2. An email with both an event and an offer (e.g. a paid event discount) is `promotion` if its main goal is selling, otherwise `meeting`.
3. If still unsure, choose `other`.
